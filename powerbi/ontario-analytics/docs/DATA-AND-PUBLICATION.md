## Original sources and rights evidence

**PHO:** [Terms of Use, sections 2, 3 and 10](https://www.publichealthontario.ca/en/About/Terms-of-Use) limit reuse and describe a permission process. No dataset-specific grant establishing public redistribution was verified in the historical workbook or source page. The repository's older claim that this dataset has an Ontario open-government licence is not carried forward as an established fact.

**CIHI:** [Terms of use, Use of CIHI Materials](https://www.cihi.ca/en/about-cihi/terms-of-use) allow specified educational/research uses but require express written authorization for inclusion in software applications. Source credit and retained notices are also required. No written authorization for this public application was supplied.

**Source pages:** [PHO snapshot](https://www.publichealthontario.ca/en/data-and-analysis/chronic-disease/chronic-disease-incidence-prevalence) and [CIHI 2025 release](https://www.cihi.ca/en/wait-times-for-priority-procedures-in-canada-2025). The historical PHO workbook credits ICES Chronic Disease Derived Cohorts; its notices must be retained in authorized local copies.

The practical publication decision is to withhold real observations from new public assets pending suitable permission. This is a project-specific review record, not a grant of rights or legal advice. Publisher names/source links identify provenance; no logos or endorsement are claimed.

## Before any real-data public release

Record written permission or an applicable dataset-specific licence, its permitted use, redistribution scope, expiry and attribution requirements. Review derived images, browser payloads, model caches, downloadable reports and repository history as well as raw inputs. Recheck both publishers' current terms before release. Do not put permission correspondence containing personal contact details in a public repository.
